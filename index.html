About these screen savers…
Original artwork, decoded Recreated in CSS by other people

The purple ones are the point: sprites lifted from the .AD modules and decoded with the same routines the savers shipped with, so pixels and palettes are exact. Behaviour follows what each module says about itself — settings, sounds, and its own description string. See tools/README.md.

The teal ones are the fork’s CSS-only recreations (no images, no JavaScript). They stay behind the checkbox above.

Full screen covers this page and stops on a click or key. New tab opens the saver alone; Embed… gives you the markup. Settings… is the module's own control panel, in its own words, and the choices go on the URL — so any of these is a link.
