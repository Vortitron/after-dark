After Dark for ESPHome Setup ×

Put the After Dark screen savers on an ESP32 with a little screen, and pick a saver from Home Assistant. Choose your board and screen, tick the savers you want, and copy the config into ESPHome. Only what you tick goes into the firmware.

Each saver is ported from its page on this site. On a 1-bit OLED its art is shrunk and redrawn in black and white; on a character LCD it is drawn with the panel's eight custom characters.

Install it with Vome
>_

If your Home Assistant is connected to Vome, your AI assistant (Claude, Cursor, or any app that talks MCP) can put this on the board for you. It adds the config to ESPHome Device Builder, builds the firmware and installs it, and you approve each step.

  1. Add the Vome app to Home Assistant and make a key on its Agent tab, as Vome's ESPHome guide shows. Home Assistant needs the ESPHome Device Builder app too.
  2. For a new board, plug it into the machine running Home Assistant by USB. After the first install, updates go over Wi-Fi.
  3. Copy the request below and paste it to your assistant.
The request carries the config above, so set up the board and screen first.
